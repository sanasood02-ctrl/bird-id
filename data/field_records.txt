Jungle babbler:
Ash-brown coloured upper body, lighter brown underbelly, smaller than a crow, with an extended blunt tail, pale eyes, a small yellow insect bill, orange feet, no neck, often seen in trees and bushes, loud chattering sound
Located: Indian subcontinent

Red-vented bulbul:
Small to medium-sized bird, black head with a small crest, pointed black beak and black eyes, darker brown wings and upper body with a white and fluffy lower body, red tuft under tail, brown tail with dark brown tail tips, often seen in trees or on wires
Location: South and Southeast Asia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Hawaii

Indian peafowl (male)
Very large ground bird, elongated iridescent neck, ornamental upper tail feathers in green and blue with complex patterning, black and white mottling and striping on wings, long hooked feet, distinctive white and black patch around eye, fan-shaped cluster crest on top of small head, often seen on the ground or flying low and hopping small distances, distinctive may-yow call sound
Location: Indian subcontinent

Plum-headed parakeet
Distinctively coloured parakeet, bright green body with long tail, yellow beak, dark neck ring around plum-coloured head (male) or yellow neck ring around blue-grey coloured head (female), small yellow beak, often found flying or in trees
Location: Indian subcontinent

Pied kingfisher
Boldly marked black and white kingfisher, small bird with long black beak and a short neck, white marks on neck and lower body, large elongated bill, mottled and striped black and white wing feathers, white and grey underside of wings and white underbelly, short black tail with white stripe, often seen flying near water bodies or diving for fish
Location: Indian subcontinent, South African subcontinent

Black Drongo
Medium-sized black bird with long forked tail, small blunt hooked black beak, brown markings near tail, grey underside of wings, small feet, often seen flying or diving, near forests or water
Location: Southeast Asia

Red-wattled lapwing
Medium-sized bird, brown wings with a white underbelly and a stripe across the upper body and wings, black neck and head with a white patch near the eye and a red patch surrounding the eye, leading into a red beak with a black tip, a black-tipped tail, long yellow legs, often seen running on the ground
Location: Indian subcontinent
